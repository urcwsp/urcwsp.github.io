About Me

I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Robotics at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Tianjin University of Technology.

My research interests lie at the intersection of reinforcement learning and autonomous driving, with a particular focus on robust decision-making and control under uncertainty.

My current work investigates adversarial reinforcement learning methods for improving the robustness and safety of autonomous driving systems under dynamic disturbances and model uncertainty.
